========================================================
SIGNALBRIDGE - EMERGENCY DISPATCH BOT INSTRUCTIONS
========================================================

1. FILE DESCRIPTIONS & SIGNIFICANCE
-----------------------------------
- main.py:
  The core application file. It initializes the Caspian SDK CommClient to listen for Telegram messages, manages the 15-second emergency state machine, handles Google Maps URL parsing, and routes the final dispatch payload.
  
- ai_extractor.py:
  The "brain" of the bot. It connects to the Groq API (using Llama 3) to analyze inbound distress messages (text or transcribed audio) and extracts the nature of the emergency, its severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low), and any mentioned location landmarks.

- mock_dispatch.py:
  Simulates a real-world emergency dispatch center (like 911). It provides a FastAPI endpoint that receives the structured emergency payload and logs it with visual sirens for debugging/dashboarding purposes.

- users.json:
  The multi-tenant database. It stores the Telegram IDs of authorized users, their pending emergency states, and the IDs of the family groups they have successfully linked to their account.

- requirements.txt:
  Lists all Python dependencies (fastapi, uvicorn, groq>=1.6.0, caspian-sdk, httpx, etc.) required to run the project.

- .env (Not tracked in version control):
  Stores sensitive environment variables, most notably your GROQ_API_KEY.


2. HOW TO SET UP THE BOT
------------------------
1. Install Dependencies:
   Open your terminal in the project folder and run:
   pip install -r requirements.txt

2. Configure API Keys:
   Ensure your `.env` file contains your Groq API key:
   GROQ_API_KEY=your_api_key_here

3. Start the Server:
   Run the Uvicorn server to launch the bot:
   uvicorn main:app --reload

   Wait until you see "Application startup complete" in the terminal.


3. HOW TO USE THE BOT IN TELEGRAM
---------------------------------
Step 1: Link Your Family Group
- Open Telegram and add the bot to a Group Chat containing your family members.
- Inside that group chat, type the secret registration password:
  ClaudeCodeIsAllUNeed
- The bot will reply confirming that the group is now successfully linked to your account for emergency alerts.

Step 2: Trigger an Emergency
- Go to the bot's Direct Messages (DM). IMPORTANT: Do not send emergencies in the group chat, only talk to the bot privately!
- Send a distress signal either by typing (e.g. "I am trapped in a fire") or by sending a Voice Note.
- The AI will analyze your message. If it detects a real emergency, it will instantly notify you and begin a 15-second auto-dispatch countdown.

Step 3: Provide Your Location
- While the 15-second timer is ticking, open the Google Maps app on your phone.
- Tap the blue dot (your location), click "Share Location", and "Copy Link".
- Paste that Google Maps link into the bot's DM. 
- (Alternatively, if you are at a famous landmark like "Eiffel Tower", you can just type it in your distress message and the bot will auto-geocode it).

Step 4: Dispatch
- As soon as you paste the link, the bot will intercept it, extract your exact GPS coordinates, cancel the 15-second timer, and immediately blast the fully structured emergency alert (with coordinates, severity, and nature of the emergency) to your linked Family Group!
- If you fail to provide a location within 15 seconds, the bot will auto-dispatch the alert anyway, marking the location as "Unknown Location" just to be safe.
